    Figures are parsed automatically from titles and abstracts, extracted by AI or checked by the lab (annotations.csv); hover over a figure to see which. Energy efficiency and channel loss are taken as reported; papers count different blocks (TX, RX, clocking, DSP) and measure loss at different frequencies, so compare with care. Values marked ≈ are power divided by data rate. When an abstract reports several values for one field (per mode or frequency), the field is left empty instead of guessing.
